A head chef, two sous chefs, two stations

Think of it as a kitchen brigade. The guest tastes and tells us what they think. Our Head Chef turns that into the dish, more sour but still airy, and assigns the work. Two sous chefs (Scientist A and B) each run their own station. Both draw on the same pantry: computed acid chemistry from NVIDIA ALCHEMI and our modeled fermentation forecasts. Each sous chef sends out a dozen line cooks, candidate simulations, and keeps the best. Then they argue across the pass until they agree on a trial. Every station is safety-locked, because firmware clamps each setpoint.

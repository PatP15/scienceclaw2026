Seven steps from sensors to printed crumb

Here’s the pipeline, step by step, and each step is labelled real or simulated. One: two chambers sense temperature, humidity, gas and rise. Two: a fermentation model simulates acidity and rise. Three: NVIDIA ALCHEMI adds real molecular evidence about the acids. Four: two AI agents debate what to try next. Five: a person tastes and types plain feedback. Six: the agreed experiment goes to the chambers, with safety limits enforced in firmware. Seven: each prediction becomes a printed crumb hypothesis, which we check against photos of the real loaf. All of it runs on a laptop, offline.

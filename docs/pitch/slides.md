# FermentAI: four-slide investor pitch (3 minutes)

The audience is investors and engineers who have never heard of the project.

**Few-words rule.** The figure carries each slide. Each slide gets:

- a headline of 8 words or fewer;
- at most 3 short on-slide bullets, or none;
- one figure with about 15 visible words, one-word badges (MEASURED / COMPUTED / MODELED), one number per tile and
  big type.

Everything else goes in the speaker notes below or in the numbers table at the end: sentences,
sources, caveats and secondary numbers. Investors read diligence, so the provenance badges stay on
every figure.

Colours are the same everywhere: Chamber A is blue `#1f77b4` and Chamber B is orange `#ff7f0e`.
Provenance badges have three tiers:

- **MEASURED** (green): from the physical world, meaning sensor telemetry, loaf photos and tasting.
  Nothing is measured yet, so where it appears it is drawn dashed or outlined, as "pending".
- **COMPUTED** (teal): a calculation that actually ran, with logs. This covers the NVIDIA ALCHEMI
  energetics (computed, and checked against published experiments) and the PreForm print prep.
- **MODELED** (purple): our own process model, meaning the fermentation forecasts, crumb geometry and
  benchmark (the benchmark is "MODELED · in-silico"). It is not yet validated against a bake.

EXAMPLE is grey. The figures live in
`data/figures/` and are regenerated by `uv run python scripts/make_pitch_figures.py`, which reads
every number from the data files. Partner names appear as plain-text wordmarks only. The deck
sources in `docs/pitch/deck/slides/*.html` mirror this file.

Timing assumes about 140 words per minute. The spoken notes total 410 words, which is
2 min 56 s (see the table at the end).

## Slide 1: problem and vision

**Headline:** Every kitchen can become a food-science lab

**Figure:** `data/figures/closed_loop.png`. It shows 8 one-word steps around "Taste → next
experiment". Ring colours: dashed green = physical steps (MEASURED, pending), purple = modeled,
slate = AI agent, crimson = the human judge.

**On-slide bullets**

- Problem: flavour by guesswork
- Vision: every kitchen a lab
- Why now: cheap GPU chemistry + AI agents

**Speaker notes (94 words, 40 s)**

> Every sourdough baker runs an experiment every day, then throws the data away. Flavour depends on
> temperature over a twelve-to-sixteen-hour ferment, and people tune it by feel. Our vision is that
> every kitchen becomes a lab, with AI scientists that run experiments, share evidence and learn from
> the people who eat the result. Why now? Molecular chemistry on a GPU costs cents, sensors cost
> dollars, and AI agents can reason and argue. This loop is the product. You bake, you taste, you
> tell the system what you think, and it designs the next experiment.

> NVIDIA ALCHEMI gives us computed molecular evidence, checked against published experiments: over a
> thousand GPU runs for a few dollars, cached so the demo runs offline. It finds lactic the stronger acid, by four pKa units against one in

## Formlabs status (for diligence questions)

The PreForm integration ran **live** on 2026-10-31. It was not a mock. Sources:

- `data/stl/print/print_manifest.json`
- `data/stl/print/preform_api_transcript.json`
- `docs/formlabs_workflow.md`
- `.progress/formlabs.md`

What happened:

- **Tooling:** the official `formlabs-sdk` 1.0.0a0.post2 drove PreFormServer 3.64.2.665 on the Mac.
- **Printer:** Form 4, Grey V5 resin, 0.1 mm layers, full raft, open crumb face up.
- **Layout:** both chamber slabs fit on one platform.
- **Estimates:** PreForm's own `calculate-print-time` and `material_usage` give 1.36 h (4,894 s), 150.24 mL of resin (models 127.05 mL + raft 21.14 mL + supports about 2 mL) and 185 layers. These are **estimates**, not a measured print.
- **Printability checks:** 7 were run.
  - Passed: in-bounds, manifold, supportedness (0 % unsupported), minima (0), interferences (none) and thin features (none).
  - Warnings: cups, 1 on A and 3 on B. These are concave pockets under pore lips. They drain because every pore opens to the cut face, but a human should review them in PreForm.
- **Outputs:** `.form` files and PreForm-rendered previews were exported.
- **Never printed:** the transcript records **426** API calls, and none of them is `/v1/scene/print`. `job_submitted` is false. Submitting needs both `--i-confirm-print` and `FERMENTAI_FORMLABS_CONFIRM_PRINT=yes`, and even then it only queues the job. A person starts the print.
- **Not food-safe:** the resin parts are display objects. Standard resins are not food-safe, so never put the prints in contact with bread.

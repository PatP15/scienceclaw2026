A head chef, two sous chefs, two stations

Think of it as a kitchen brigade. The guest tastes and tells Sous Chef A what they think. The Head Chef sets the dish, more sour but still airy, and sends out seventeen line cooks: candidate simulations. The two sous chefs (Scientist A and B), each running their own station, argue over the results across the pass. They draw on the same pantry: computed acid chemistry from NVIDIA ALCHEMI and our modeled fermentation forecasts. When they agree on a trial, each station gets its schedule. Every station is safety-locked, because firmware clamps each setpoint.

Two partners: NVIDIA chemistry, Formlabs fabrication

Two technology partners make this credible. NVIDIA ALCHEMI gives us real molecular evidence: over a thousand runs on a Brev GPU for about six dollars, cached so the demo runs offline. A thermodynamic cycle puts lactic acid about four pKa units stronger than acetic. Experiment says about one, so the sign is right, and the estimate converges as the clusters grow. That bounds the model’s acid terms, but it doesn’t drive the bread. Formlabs makes the prediction physical. The real PreForm API laid out both crumb specimens and our two chef hats on one Form 4 plate in clear resin: about two and a half hours. A person presses print.

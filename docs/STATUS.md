## Hardware bring-up next steps

Physical commissioning follows **`firmware/docs/BRINGUP.md`**, one chamber at a time, with the
results logged in its "Bring-up log". The firmware workstream owns `firmware/`, and the hardware
session owns `configs/hardware.yaml`.

1. **Step 2, reset-level check (do this first; it decides the pin map).** With no loads connected,
   measure **GPIO40 (Peltier EN)** and **GPIO38 (motor gate)** while RST is held and during the
   first second after release. Both must read **LOW (< 0.5 V)**.
   - If either is pulled high (the SD-card pull-up on some ESP32-S3-CAM boards), build with
     `-DFERMENTAI_PINMAP_ALT=1` or remove the pull-up.
   - Also check that GPIO14/21 (PWM) read LOW with the 10 kΩ pull-downs fitted.
   - Record the results in `firmware/docs/GPIO_MAP.md` §5.
2. Steps 3–8: hardware firmware with no peripherals, then the door reed switch, BME680/SHT31,
   DS18B20 probes one at a time, pH (chamber A only) and the camera field of view.
3. Step 9: motor at low power. `arm motor` is required, and the motor must never re-run after a
   reboot.
4. Step 10, Peltier, staged:
   - logic only, with no 12 V;
   - a dummy load;
   - the real Peltier, supervised;
   - a long run.

   The Peltier stays **double-locked**: compile flag `FERMENTAI_ALLOW_PELTIER_OUTPUT=0` by default,
   and the NVS `arm peltier` flag. It needs both heatsink probes.
5. Step 11: network and offline behaviour on the travel router. Then run `scripts/offline_check.sh`
   and the physical checklist in `docs/acceptance_tests.md`. Then repeat PLAN §16 items 5, 6 and 10
   with `source=measured`.

Wiring, pins and open questions are covered in `firmware/docs/SENSOR_WIRING.md`, `GPIO_MAP.md` and
`HARDWARE_UNCERTAINTIES.md`.

```bash
#   or: scripts/start_demo.sh                            # real ESP32s on the LAN
# iPad: open the printed  http://<mac-ip>:8000/?token=<FERMENTAI_API_TOKEN>

```

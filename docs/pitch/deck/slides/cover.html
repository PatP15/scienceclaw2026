Every kitchen can become a food‑science lab

  • Problem: flavour by guesswork
  • Vision: every kitchen a lab
  • Why now: cheap GPU chemistry + AI agents
Every sourdough baker runs an experiment every day, then throws the data away. Flavour depends on temperature over a twelve-to-sixteen-hour ferment, and people tune it by feel. Our vision is that every kitchen becomes a lab, with AI scientists that run experiments, share evidence and learn from the people who eat the result. Why now? Molecular chemistry on a GPU costs cents, sensors cost dollars, and AI agents can reason and argue. This loop is the product. You bake, you taste, you tell the system what you think, and it designs the next experiment.

Two partners: NVIDIA chemistry, Formlabs fabrication

NVIDIA ALCHEMI gives us real molecular evidence: over a thousand GPU runs for about six dollars, cached so the demo runs offline. It finds lactic the stronger acid, by four pKa units against one in experiment, and it moves toward experiment as we add water. That only bounds the model’s acid terms. The model itself takes the recipe, the temperature schedule and the starter’s state, and predicts pH, acids, gas, rise and overproofing. Formlabs makes the prediction physical. The real PreForm API laid out both crumb specimens and our two chef hats on one Form 4 plate: about two and a half hours. A person presses print.

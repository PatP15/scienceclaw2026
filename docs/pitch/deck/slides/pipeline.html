Two AI scientists, one experiment at a time

Each chamber has its own AI scientist. Both read the same evidence: computed molecular chemistry from NVIDIA ALCHEMI, forecasts from our fermentation model, and whatever the judges said about the last loaf. Then they argue. A proposes a hot start and a cool finish; B objects that it overproofs and counters with a steady twenty-four. They settle on trial two, and each sends a new temperature schedule down to its own chamber. The chamber firmware clamps every setpoint, so no model and no judge can push the hardware past its limits. Everything runs on a laptop, offline.
